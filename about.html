About Adv. Meera Kulkarni

Adv. Meera Kulkarni is a dedicated legal professional committed to providing strategic, ethical, and client-focused legal representation. Over more than a decade of practice, she has built a reputation for thorough preparation, clear communication, and steady advocacy across criminal, civil, corporate, family and property matters.

Her practice is guided by the belief that every client, regardless of the size or complexity of their matter, deserves careful attention, honest counsel, and a legal strategy tailored to their specific circumstances.
